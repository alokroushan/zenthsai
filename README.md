# Zenth: Reddit for the AI Generation

Zenth is an **AI-powered** social discovery platform that transforms AI image creation from a solo workflow into a shared, community‑driven experience. 

## 🚀 What is Zenth?

- A social feed where users can generate, post, and remix AI content in one place. 
- Built for AI creators, not generic social media, so the entire experience is optimized for prompts, models, and visual experimentation.  

## 🧩 Problem we’re solving

- AI image creation is isolated and fragmented across private tools and Discord bots. 
- There is no unified space to instantly share, discuss, remix, and collaborate on AI content. 
- Mainstream social platforms are cluttered with non‑AI content and lack creator‑centric tools, breaking the creative‑to‑social loop. 

## 💡 Our solution

- Zenth combines creation and community in a single social feed powered by AI.
- Users can:
  - Generate AI images directly inside the feed.
  - Post instantly, receive feedback, and iterate collaboratively.
  - Discover trending prompts, styles, and creators. [conversation_history:1]  
- In short, Zenth turns AI into a shared creative culture that is social, interactive, and community‑driven.
